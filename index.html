The SheCycled Way

We combine climate education in schools with research on turning agricultural waste into sanitary pads, so learning and dignity grow together for young people, women and girls in northern Nigeria.

⚙

Climate education

We teach students about climate change and waste management in schools in Jos and Adamawa State, so they can act in their own communities.

🌿

Waste to sanitary pads

Our completed research, funded by Genetti Eco, explored how agricultural waste can be turned into sanitary pads.

☀

Menstrual health

We train women and girls in menstrual health and hygiene management and give out pads after each session.

Why we’re building SheCycled

SheCycled started in 2024 in northern Nigeria, where insecurity has pushed many families into IDP camps. Women and girls there need safe menstrual products, and agricultural waste is available to make them. We teach climate education in schools and are working to turn that waste into sanitary pads.

Our core SDGs

Our work supports five UN Sustainable Development Goals. Here is how.

SDG 4

Climate education in schools

Our climate education has reached 3,000 students across schools in Jos and Adamawa State.

SDG 3

Menstrual health training

We have distributed over 2,000 sanitary pads to girls while teaching them menstrual health.

SDG 5

Dignity for women and girls

We are working to give women and girls in IDP camps safe pads and the knowledge to manage their health.

SDG 12

Waste into a useful product

We are turning agricultural waste into sanitary pads instead of letting it go unused.

SDG 13

Climate action from the classroom

Students learn practical waste management and climate action they can apply at home.
